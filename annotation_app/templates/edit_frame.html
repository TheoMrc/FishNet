Instructions

Head mode:

  • Shift + click to add a head
  • Click to select a head
  • Delete to remove the selected head
  • Z / S / Q / D to move the selected head

Midline mode:

  • Shift + click to add a point
  • Click to select and move a point
  • Alt + click the head to move it
  • Delete to remove the selected point
  • Arrow right / left to navigate fish
  • Space toggles point visibility
